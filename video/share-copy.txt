CodeCloud is an open-source, self-hosted platform for learning DevOps on real machines: 20 courses and 21 auto-graded labs that boot a private Linux box, Docker engine or Kubernetes cluster right in the browser, and check your work against the machine's real state. edX courses come in, Open edX exports go out, and GitHub Actions boots and solves every lab nightly — `docker compose up -d` and it's yours.
